# 🌍 Sastermate 

**A City Management & Climate Crisis Strategy Game**  
*Developed for the COP31 Game Jam (2026)*

![Sastermate Main Menu](https://via.placeholder.com/800x400?text=Sastermate+Main+Menu+Image+Here)

## 📖 About the Game
The year is 2026. The effects of climate change have become severe, and unplanned urbanization has left cities vulnerable to climate hazards. You are the newly appointed **COP31 Representative** for the city of Nova. 

On your very first day, a massive fire disaster breaks out. Your mission is not just to intervene in emergencies, but to make Nova resilient and sustainable. You must manage your limited budget (Carbon Points), fight disasters, and invest in green infrastructure to keep the global temperature increase strictly below **+2.0°C**. If the temperature rises too high, the city is lost!

## ✨ Key Features
- **Real-time Crisis Management:** Act fast! Click to extinguish sudden fires spreading across the city to prevent temperature spikes and earn Carbon Points (CP).
- **Green Economy System:** Spend your hard-earned CP on sustainable solutions like **Solar Panels**, **Afforestation**, and **Recycling Centers** to permanently lower the city's temperature.
- **Stylized Isometric 3D World:** Experience the city of Nova in a beautiful, child-friendly cel-shaded art style with dynamic Cinemachine camera movements.
- **Educational Impact:** Designed to raise awareness among children and young adults about global warming, renewable energy, and sustainable living.

## 🎮 How to Play
1. **Watch the Temperature:** The main UI features a temperature slider (0.0°C - 2.0°C). Don't let it max out!
2. **Put out Fires:** Fires will randomly spawn on buildings and trees. Douse them quickly to earn CP.
3. **Spend CP Wisely:** Open the "Spend CP" menu and invest in:
   - ☀️ **Solar Panels (50 CP):** Generate green energy.
   - 🌲 **Afforestation (30 CP):** Plant trees to lower the overall temperature.
   - ♻️ **Recycling (45 CP):** Process waste to improve city resilience.
4. **Save the City:** Balance your immediate crisis response with long-term ecological investments to achieve Victory!

## 🛠️ Built With
- **Engine:** Unity 6.3 (Universal Render Pipeline - URP)
- **Language:** C#
- **Key Packages:** Cinemachine (for dynamic menu and gameplay cameras), TextMeshPro.

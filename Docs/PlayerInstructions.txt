HOW TO PLAY

Survive for 3 minutes without the temperature reaching +2 degrees Celsius.

WASD / arrow keys: move
Mouse: aim the hose
Left mouse button: spray water
Q / E: rotate the camera
Upgrades button: open the upgrade shop
Close / X: close the upgrade shop
Escape: close the upgrade shop, or pause/back during gameplay

Extinguish fires to earn Carbon Points (CP). Spend CP on solar panels,
recycling, and tree planting to slow future warming. You cannot spray
while the upgrade shop is open. Release and press the mouse again after closing it.

Finish the countdown below +2 degrees to win. Reaching +2 degrees loses.
Use Play Again on the result screen for a fresh round.
